A practical guide to cloud continuity in Saudi Arabia

A decision framework for infrastructure leaders evaluating resilience, recovery and operational ownership.

Published: 15 July 2026 · 8 minute read · By TopNet

Context

Cloud continuity begins with a shared understanding of critical workloads, business dependencies and the level of disruption each service can tolerate. Infrastructure leaders need recovery plans that connect technical design with operational ownership.

“A recovery plan is only credible when responsibilities, dependencies and testing evidence are visible.”

Evaluation framework

Structure the evaluation around recovery objectives, dependency mapping, data protection, operating responsibility, testing cadence, regulatory obligations and measurable service levels.

Key checklist

  • Define workload criticality and ownership
  • Validate recovery and backup requirements
  • Confirm security, compliance and escalation expectations
